# Rights and dependency ledger for candidate 0.1.0

**Historical candidate ledger:** the [current ten-step AGPL MVP plan](AGPL_MVP_TEN_STEP_PLAN.md) expands the public messaging scope and replaces the self-imposed external-review policy for the next release. Confirmed first-party authority carries forward. Statements below about a mandatory qualified reviewer describe the earlier candidate process, not a condition imposed by AGPL. Future implementation must extend this ledger and update the live audit; frozen candidate records remain unchanged.

This is an engineering inventory, not a legal certification. On October 24, 2026, the owner expressly confirmed: “Yes, I control the first-party rights.” That confirmation is accepted for first-party licensing authority. It does not establish ownership of third-party material or resolve license compatibility/the concrete combination boundary. Publication remains blocked pending qualified review and approval of the exact release. Current package declarations are not changed by extraction. Prior rights already granted remain intact.
